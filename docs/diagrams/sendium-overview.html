Scope, technical accuracy and design sources

This is an integration overview, not a protocol sequence or deployment topology. Both ingress protocols can route to either configured upstream provider; row alignment does not assign HTTP traffic to A or SMPP traffic to B. Routing selects a configured destination; arrows do not imply broadcast, load balancing, outage detection or automatic failover.

OTP generation, notification logic and campaign management remain in the originating applications. Sendium bridges message protocols and manages routing and throughput controls; it does not supply provider connectivity contracts or worldwide coverage. Delivery receipts depend on client requests, provider support, correlation, persistence and return-path configuration. Submission acceptance is not delivery confirmation.

Sources: HTTP APIs, SMPP and throughput configuration, routing rules, callbacks and DLR persistence. Queues, storage, credentials, binds, acknowledgements, retries and deployment infrastructure are omitted for clarity.

Design: diagram-design Architecture grammar and minimal-light template, doc-wide canvas, mixed audience. Brand tokens sampled from sendium.org: navy #000050, yellow #FACA16 and white #FFFFFF. Helvetica/Arial are deliberate offline substitutes for Inter Tight headings. No remote fonts, scripts or assets are loaded. Five components and eight connectors.
